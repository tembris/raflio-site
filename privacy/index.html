§2. Scope

Raflio is a mobile app offering personalized book recommendations, a virtual bookshelf, book logging/rating, and (optionally) social features such as following other readers. This policy explains what personal data we process when you use the App, why, with whom it may be shared, how long it is kept, and what rights you have.

This policy covers only the App itself. It does not cover the App Store's or Google Play's own data practices, which are governed by Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies.

§3. What Personal Data We Process

Each category below is only created when you actually use the corresponding feature.

§3.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device — Your Bookshelf

When you add a book, rate it, change its reading status (read/reading/DNF/unread), position it on your virtual shelf, or add a private note, this data is stored primarily on your device:

  • Your shelf list, ratings, reading status, shelf/row/position placement
  • 🔒 Your private notes — visible only to you, never shown to any other user or in any server-side public view
  • Free-text labels you attach to books yourself

How it is protected: this data is stored on your device encrypted with AES-256 (via react-native-mmkv's encrypted storage mode). The encryption key is not stored alongside the data — it lives in your operating system's own secure enclave: Keychain on iOS, Keystore/EncryptedSharedPreferences on Android. This means that even if your device is lost, stolen, or its lock is bypassed, your shelf data and private notes cannot be read as plain text.

§3.2 Account Information

When you create an account (email/password, Google sign-in, or Sign in with Apple), we process:

  • Your email address (if you registered by email) or the identity token your provider (Google/Apple) shares with us — we never see your password
  • Your internal system identifier (a UUID) — all other data is linked to this identifier, not to your email address
  • Information you choose to show on your profile: username (@handle), display name, a short bio, an avatar color (a color code, not a photo), your chosen shelf theme, and a privacy toggle controlling whether your profile is public or followers-only

Your session tokens are stored in your device's secure vault (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and are never kept as plain text.

§3.3 Shared Book Catalog Entries

If a book you're looking for isn't in our catalog, you may add it by typing its title and (optionally) author. This is catalog data, not personal data about you, but it works as follows and you should be aware of it:

  • The title/author you enter is added to our shared, public book catalog and becomes searchable by every other user
  • The entry itself does not display who added it to other users, but our system does internally record who submitted it, for abuse prevention and moderation review
  • Submitted text goes through length and character validation to prevent technical abuse (e.g. spreadsheet formula injection); invalid input is rejected

§3.4 Social Features and User-Generated Content

If you use social features (following another user, posting a public review, blocking someone, reporting content), we process:

  • The identities of users you follow / who follow you
  • Your public reviews (review) — shown on the book's detail page, visible to anyone. 🔒 This is different from your private notes in §3.1 — your notes are never public; your reviews always are
  • The identities of users you block (visible only to you)
  • When you report content/a user: the reported content, your selected reason (chosen from a predefined category list), and the report timestamp — reviewed by our moderation process

If you set your profile to private, your shelf and reviews are visible only to followers you approve.

§3.5 Analytics (Usage) Data

We plan to use a product analytics tool called PostHog to improve the App. Once enabled, it will collect: which screens/features you use (e.g. "you rated a book", "a recommendation row was shown"), technical information such as device/OS/app version, and your internal system identifier (UUID). Book titles, author names, the text of your notes or reviews, and your email address are never sent to this tool — only the book's internal ID (book_id) and fixed, predefined category values (enums) are processed.

📌 Current status (as of this policy's effective date): the PostHog software component is bundled with the App but is disabled at the network level — it sends nothing. In development builds, these events are only logged to the developer's own local console; in production builds the call is a silent no-op. In other words, no data is currently actually collected via PostHog. When this tool is enabled, this policy will be updated and the change will be announced in-app and/or via a store listing update.

§3.6 Crash and Diagnostic Data

Crash reporting is OFF in v1 — no diagnostic data leaves your device, not even when the App crashes. The App contains Sentry code, but no connection address (DSN) is shipped in the production build and the crash reporting code is explicitly disabled.

If this is ever enabled, it would collect: a technical description of the error (stack trace), device model, OS version, and app version. Your email address and IP address are automatically stripped before such data is sent (a verified filtering step in our code). Any such change would be announced under §11.

§3.7 Purchase Data

Premium is a one-time purchase (not a subscription). The purchase itself is processed by Google Play — your payment/card details never reach us. To verify whether Premium is unlocked on this device we use a service called RevenueCat; through it only your purchase history is processed (whether you bought Premium, its price and currency). This data is linked to an anonymous device identifier generated by RevenueCat — not to your name, email, library, ratings or notes, because the App sends none of these to that service.

§3.8 AI-Assisted Catalog Enrichment — a Process Your Personal Data Never Enters

Catalog metadata such as book summaries and categories are generated in a batch, server-side process using the Google Gemini model (with Anthropic Claude as a fallback provider). Only the book's title and author (catalog data) are sent to this process.

🔒 Clarification: this process has nothing to do with your personal data. Your notes, reviews, ratings, reading history, taste profile, or any identifying information are never sent to these AI providers. The personalized recommendation calculation (which book fits you) runs entirely on your own device and is never sent to any server or AI provider.

Some factual catalog fields (publication year, page count, publisher) may also be supplemented from Open Library, an open data source; this source contains no personal data, only public book information.

§3.9 Backup and Export Data

When you export your shelf as CSV/JSON (this feature is free for everyone), the resulting file includes all of your shelf data, including your private notes and reviews. This file is created temporarily on your device and shared by you, through the share sheet you choose — we do not see where it goes, and it is never sent to our servers. The file may remain briefly in your device's temporary/cache storage after sharing.

§3.10 Notifications

In-app reminders (e.g. "you have books waiting on your shelf") are scheduled entirely locally, on your device. There is no server-side notification campaign, and no push token is collected for this purpose.

§3.11 What We Do Not Collect

To be explicit — the App never requests or collects: your location (GPS), camera/photos, microphone, contacts, biometric data (fingerprint/face data stays within your device's own OS and is never shared with us), advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), or third-party ad tracking data. The App contains no advertising.

§4. Purposes of Processing

Your personal data is processed to:

  1. Create and authenticate your account and maintain your session
  2. Sync your shelf, ratings, and reading status across your devices (if you use more than one)
  3. Provide personalized book recommendations (computed on your device)
  4. Operate social features (following, reviews, activity feed)
  5. Protect the platform from abuse (spam, harassment, inappropriate content); review reported content and remove it or suspend accounts when warranted
  6. Manage subscriptions and purchases
  7. Detect and fix bugs (crash reporting)
  8. Improve the product using aggregated usage trends (once §3.5 is enabled)
  9. Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

§7. Retention

  • Account and shelf data: retained while your account is active. When you delete your account (§9), your associated shelf, social relationships (follows/blocks), and report records are automatically deleted at the database level (verified: cascading deletion is configured on account deletion).
  • Local device data: retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Shared catalog entries (§3.3): a book's presence in the catalog is not tied to your personal account — deleting your account does not necessarily remove a book you added, since it is now a shared, public catalog record with no personal attribution shown to other users.
  • Analytics data (§3.5): none is collected in v1 — the analytics tool is not wired into the code. Once enabled, raw person-linked event data will be retained for 90 days, after which only aggregate counts that cannot be linked to a person are kept.
  • Crash/error logs: not collected at all in v1 (§3.6). If enabled, they would be subject to Sentry's default retention policy.

§8. Security Measures

  • Your shelf data and private notes are encrypted on-device with AES-256; the encryption key is stored in your OS's hardware-backed secure vault (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), separately from the data itself.
  • On the server, every database table is protected with row-level access control (Row Level Security) — it is structurally impossible at the database layer for one user to read another user's private notes, not merely a choice made in app code.
  • All network traffic is encrypted via HTTPS; unencrypted (http://) connections are not used.
  • Your session tokens are stored in your device's secure vault, never as plain text.
  • Rate limiting is applied to abuse-prone endpoints.
  • When you block a user, that user's content becomes completely invisible to you at the database query level.

No system is 100% secure. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures but cannot guarantee absolute security.

§9. Children's Data

Raflio includes user-generated content (reviews, profiles, following) and is therefore not directed at children under 13, and the "Kids" store category has not been selected. If we learn we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will delete the account. If you are a parent/guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, contact us using §12.

§10. Your GDPR Rights

If you are in the EU/EEA/UK, you have the right to: access your data (Art. 15), rectify it (Art. 16), erase it / be forgotten (Art. 17), restrict processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20 — already fulfilled by the free export feature in §3.9), object to processing (Art. 21), and lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

§11. KVKK Notice for Users in Türkiye

Since Türkiye is our primary market, users there are additionally entitled to the rights under KVKK art. 11, including the right to: learn whether your data is processed; request information about it; learn its purpose and whether it is used accordingly; know third parties it is transferred to (domestically or abroad); request correction of inaccurate data; request erasure/destruction once the conditions of KVKK art. 7 are met; request that such correction/erasure be notified to third parties the data was transferred to; object to a result that is to your detriment arising solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing. The full Turkish-language disclosure is provided in the Turkish version of this policy (raflio.app/gizlilik), which governs for Turkish-language users and in case of conflict.
